# Raasta: product

**Problem (Rotary x Rotaract "Naya Bharat 2047, Code for Bharat", Problem 1.2, Peacebuilding and Conflict Prevention).**
When people face an everyday dispute (deposit not returned, faulty product, a neighbour's construction cracking a wall, unpaid wages, UPI fraud, a pothole) they don't know which office handles it: police, consumer commission, labour office, ward office, Lok Adalat, free legal aid. Many give up or rely on whoever they know. Small unresolved disputes harden into lasting conflict between neighbours and families.

**Challenge.** Help people quickly understand which authority or service handles their specific problem and what their first step is, often a free and peaceful one, before it escalates.

**Users.** Ordinary residents, many on old phones and slow networks, in 12 languages. Pune gets ward-level depth; the rest of India gets central, sectoral and state routes.

**What we do.** Guided questions or a typed description lead to one deterministic route: the first peaceful step, the right door, documents, deadlines, legal-aid eligibility, nearby offices, and a ready draft (PDF, email, WhatsApp, postal label, or direct API filing where a real API exists).

**Evidence on hand.** Only figures with a cited source (DAKSH survey: 74% turn first to friends and family, 49% to village elders; police 40% and lawyers 32% top the channels people avoid). Registry data in packages/data/source.

**Must not be fabricated.** Counters, testimonials, logos, portal screenshots, URLs, fees, phone numbers, deadlines. Raasta gives procedural guidance, not legal advice.
